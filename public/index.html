Territory Siege

Defend your core. Grow your land. Siege your neighbours.

  • Buy units (Guard, Archer, Frost, Mortar, Medic) that patrol your core and beacons. More land = bigger army.
  • Beacons expand your land (more land = more gold), but creeps raid them - assign guards!
  • Send creeps at other players. Destroying a core pays a big bounty.
  • Leapfrog beacons past your border and park units on enemy beacons to capture them.
  • Fallen bases leave ruins you can capture cheaply.
