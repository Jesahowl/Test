How to play

🏰 WavesEvery 20s your core sends 4 troops (one of each type), plus 4 from every barracks. Choose the mix in the wave panel. It's free; gold buys levels.
🎯 SquadsEvery wave marches out as a squad that sticks together. Click one of its troops (or press its number), then right-click a target.
🗺 LandThe map is made of hexes. Claim them with gold (L, then click or drag along your border). More land means more gold and a bigger army. Towers defend it.
🏆 WinTake enemy towers, break their core, and be the last core standing. Beating a barbarian Warlord powers up your troops.
