How to play

🏰 ArmyYou have up to 4 squads. Choose each squad's troops in Squads (C). Troops that fall respawn every 20s and rejoin their squad. Land and barracks make your army bigger; gold buys levels.
🎯 SquadsClick one of a squad's troops (or press 1–4), then right-click a target. Set each squad's stance: aggressive, defensive or hold.
🗺 LandThe map is made of hexes. Claim them with gold (L, then click or drag along your border). More land means more gold and a bigger army. Towers defend it.
🏆 WinTake enemy towers, break their core, and be the last core standing. Beating a barbarian Warlord powers up your troops.
