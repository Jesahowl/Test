How to play

🏰 WavesEvery 20s your core sends 5 troops, plus 5 from every barracks. Choose the mix in the wave panel. It's free; gold buys levels.
🎯 OrdersRight-click an enemy tower, core, camp or troop to attack it, or your own building to defend it. The Send slider decides how many go.
🗺 LandTowers claim land and shoot. More land means more gold. Explore the fog with scouts.
🏆 WinTake enemy towers, break their core, and be the last core standing. Beating a barbarian Warlord powers up your troops.
