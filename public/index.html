How to play

🏰 ArmyYour core raises squad 1. Each barracks raises another squad (up to 4); upgrade it for more troops.
🎯 OrdersPick a squad, then right-click a tower, core or camp. Fallen troops rejoin; a squad wiped out takes a while.
🗺 LandBuy hexes at your border with gold. More land, more gold. Towers guard it.
⛳ ObjectivesLandmarks between the bases (Gold Mine, Watchtower, Shrine of War, Healing Spring, Barracks Ruins). Stand troops in one's hex to take it; it works while you hold it (3 at most).
🏆 WinBreak enemy cores. The last core standing wins (or the most land when time runs out).
