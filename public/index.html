Territory Siege

Send your waves. Take their towers. Break their core.

  • Your core sends out a wave of troops every 20 seconds. Spend gold to make waves bigger and stronger.
  • Right-click an enemy tower or core to attack it, or one of yours to defend it.
  • Towers claim land and shoot. More land = more gold and a bigger army.
  • Clear jungle camps for gold. Golem camps also empower your troops for 60s.
  • Last core standing wins the round. After 10 minutes, sudden death makes sure it ends.
